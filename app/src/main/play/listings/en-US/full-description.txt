Time to hit the shed.

Woodshed is a practice app for jazz musicians of all skill levels. It plans your practice around the tunes you are learning: it brings each one back when it is due, in a key and with an exercise to work on, and fills the rest of a session with tone, technique and a solo to transcribe. It is free and open source, with no account, no ads and no tracking.

2,522 STANDARDS
Real Books 1 to 6 and the Real Dixieland Book, with composers, forms and researched keys. Search and filter them, add your own tunes, and fix anything that is wrong.

SPACED REPETITION
Each tune comes back when it is due, from the wishlist through learning to repertoire. Woodshed tracks the keys you know each tune in, and can take a tune round the circle of fourths.

PRACTICE SESSIONS
Pick a length, your instrument and what to focus on: tone, articulation, speed, time, harmony, ear, improvisation or reading. Woodshed plans a warm-up, exercises, a solo and tunes, and you can swap or drop any block. Sessions keep time with the screen off, with a notification to move on, pause or end.

TRANSCRIBE BY EAR
Keep a list of solos to learn. Each one plays from just before it starts, at half or three-quarter speed, with five-second skips and an A-B loop for the phrase you are working out.

WRITTEN FOR YOUR INSTRUMENT
Choose from 26 instruments. Keys, the tuner and the drone are named for its transposition, and exercises are picked to suit it.

CHANGES
Blues, standard forms, cadences, turnarounds and key-to-key patterns, or changes you write yourself, played in time in any key, with the chord now and next. A harmony session plays some too.

METRONOME, TUNER AND DRONE
A sample-accurate metronome with tap tempo, jazz tempo presets and clicks on 2 and 4; a tuner; and a drone that plays a note or a chord, voiced close, spread or as a shell. The metronome and drone keep playing during a session.

AND MORE
Roll a random tune, exercise and key, a solo, or changes when you would rather not plan. Make playlists for a gig or a lesson. Attach recordings to any tune, with YouTube and Vimeo playing in place.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
Everything lives on your phone. Back it up to a file wherever you like and restore it on another phone. Woodshed goes online only to play a video you tap and to send a problem report you choose to send.
