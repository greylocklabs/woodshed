Woodshed the everything app for jazz musicians of all skill levels

Get the standards under your fingers.

Woodshed plans your practice around the tunes you are learning: it brings each one back when it is due, in a key and with an exercise to work on, and fills the rest of a session with tone, technique and a solo to transcribe. Free, open source, with no account and no ads.

What it does

  • 2,522 standardsReal Books 1 to 6 and the Real Dixieland Book, with composers, forms and researched keys. Add your own tunes, and fix anything that is wrong.
  • Spaced repetitionEach tune comes back when it is due, from the wishlist through learning to repertoire, with the keys you know it in and practice round the circle of fourths.
  • Practice sessionsPick a length, an instrument and what to focus on. Woodshed plans warm-up, exercises, a solo and tunes, and keeps time with the screen off.
  • Transcribe by earSolos play from just before they start, at half or three-quarter speed, with an A-B loop for the phrase you are working out.
  • Written for your instrumentChoose from 26 instruments. Keys, the tuner and the drone are named for its transposition, and exercises suit it.
  • Metronome, tuner and droneTap tempo, jazz tempo presets and clicks on 2 and 4; a tuner; and a drone with an optional fifth.
  • Roll the diceA random tune, exercise and key, or a solo, when you would rather not plan.
  • Playlists and recordingsLists for a gig or a lesson, and recordings on any tune, with YouTube and Vimeo playing in place.
  • Your data stays yoursEverything lives on your phone. Back it up to a file wherever you like, and restore it on another.

Install

Woodshed is for Android 8 and later. Download the APK from the latest release and open it on your phone; Android asks once to allow installs from your browser or file manager. Each release installs over the last, keeping your data. Google Play is coming.

Support Woodshed

Woodshed is free, with no subscription and nothing to sell. If it helps your practice, you can support its development with a coffee on Ko-fi.

What it does not do

Woodshed has no account, no ads and no analytics. It works offline. It goes online only to play a video you tap and to send a problem report you choose to send, and the report says nothing about you. Read the privacy policy for the details.
